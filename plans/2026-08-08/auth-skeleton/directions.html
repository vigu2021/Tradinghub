What I'd actually do

Keep Terminal Ledger and steal one idea from Split Proof. The direction you have is already the one that survives contact with slice 2: monospace with tabular figures is what a table of 400 trades needs, and amber leaves green and red free for profit and loss — which every other direction here spends on chrome.

Two changes worth making. Turn the grid down — it is doing decoration work on an empty auth screen and will fight a data table later; halve its opacity and let the ground breathe. And put one real journal entry beside the register form, the way direction 10 does. It is the cheapest version of the 26% finding, it shows what the product is for before anyone commits, and unlike a fake equity curve it is honest.

The direction I would reject hardest is Soft Fintech — not because it is bad, but because it is the one you would not be able to pick out of a lineup of ten other products a year from now.
